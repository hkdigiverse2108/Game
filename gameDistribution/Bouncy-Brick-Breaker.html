Simple Goal

Your main goal in Bouncy Brick Breaker is to bounce off platforms and climb upwards while shattering obstacles and avoiding spikes. You must collect items and avoid falling down to keep the session active for as long as possible.

To reach the highest scores, focus on grabbing power-ups like lasers to shoot bricks, shields to protect you from falling, and magnets to pull coins. Professional players always look ahead to plan their bounces and prevent early drops.

How to Play

Use the left/right arrow keys or A/D keyboard letters to steer the ball horizontally. The ball wraps around the edges of the screen, so you can move past the left edge to appear on the right side and vice versa.

Land on bricks to bounce upwards. Normal bricks shatter in one bounce, cracked bricks require two bounces, and exploding bricks blow up adjacent elements. Be careful not to land on spiked bricks, and press Spacebar to fire lasers once collected!

Tips for Success

Avoid spiked bricks at all costs. Landing on a spiked block will result in an instant game over unless you have an active energy shield protecting you. Unbreakable blocks are safe to bounce off but will not yield any points or powerups.

Use edge wrapping strategically to bypass hard blocks. When lasers are active, hold Spacebar to clear a path of bricks directly above you. Save your shields for high-hazard areas where spikes are crowded together.
