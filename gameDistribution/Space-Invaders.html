Space Invaders

Space Invaders is a neon retro reimagining of the iconic 1978 arcade shoot-'em-up. Steer your defense starship, fire powerful laser beams, and defend Earth against waves of descending alien grids.

This version brings vibrant modern neon particles, interactive barriers, multiple escalating difficulty levels, power-ups (shields, 2x point multipliers), high-fidelity sound effects, and a persistent local leaderboard so you can record your name and track your high scores.

Designed from the ground up for responsiveness, it automatically fits the screen and includes full touchscreen controls (Left, Right, Fire buttons) for mobile web browsers.

Objective

Blast all descending alien invaders before they reach the bottom of the screen or destroy your ship.

Use shields, collect score multipliers, and rack up points to climb to the top of the leaderboard!

Reviewer Rating: 4.8/5 Stars

How to Play

On desktop, press **A** / **D** or **Left** / **Right** Arrow keys to move left and right. Press **Spacebar** to shoot lasers.

On mobile devices, use the on-screen left/right arrow buttons to move and the center explosion button to fire.

Tips for Success

Use the green defensive bunker shields to protect yourself from alien bombs, but shoot carefully since your own lasers can also wear down your cover.

Prioritize the top-row invaders as they yield higher points. Keep an eye out for the red motherships flying across the top for large bonus points!
