Snake

Retro Neon Snake is a modern, beautifully polished arcade remake of the legendary Nokia Snake game. It features responsive neon visuals, smooth grid-based movement, particle explosion effects, and satisfying sound effects.

Guide the neon snake around the board to eat glowing apples. Every time the snake eats an apple, it grows in length and the game speed increases slightly, raising the stakes.

With support for touch controls, mobile swipe gestures, and keyboard navigation, this version of Snake is built to be playable on any desktop, tablet, or smartphone device. Enjoy retro arcade goodness with a high-tech coat of paint!

Objective

Eat as many glowing neon apples as you can to score points and grow your snake.

Avoid colliding with the four borders of the play field or running into the snake's own growing body!

Reviewer Rating: 4.9/5 Stars

How to Play

**Keyboard**: Use the **Arrow keys** or **WASD** to change the snake's direction.

**Mobile/Tablet**: Swipe in the direction you want the snake to go, or tap the on-screen D-pad buttons below the game board.

Tips for Success

Keep your snake aligned close to the walls as you get longer. This keeps the center of the board clear and gives you more space to maneuver.

Never make sudden 180-degree turn attempts, and plan your route one step ahead to ensure your tail doesn't block your exit path.
