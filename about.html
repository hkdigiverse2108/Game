Our Story

Epic Games Hub was founded in 2024 by Pramit Mangukia (main project handler) and Krishgiria (tester), along with software developer Kenil Lukhi (latest updater), a team of creators who share an intense passion for gaming. Tired of playing browser games hidden behind long download screens, intrusive account setups, and heavy payload launchers, they decided to build a fast, modern, and direct-to-play gaming aggregate that respects the player's time and safety.

Operating from Surat, Gujarat, the Epic Games Hub team works daily to curate, optimize, and test each web title in our library. We strive to provide a premium user experience featuring gorgeous dark-mode interfaces, smooth custom controls, and glassmorphic designs that elevate browser gaming to a console-quality feel.
